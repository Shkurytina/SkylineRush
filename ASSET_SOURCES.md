# Art and licensing

All objects currently present in the playable world are authored procedurally with built-in Roblox `Part`, `PointLight`, `BillboardGui`, `Material` and geometry primitives. No off-platform 3D assets, textures, or sounds are embedded. The project runs without network asset requests or permissions for external meshes.

Optional third-party replacement packs **not bundled**:

- Kenney Nature Kit — CC0, https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit
- Kenney Building Kit — verify the exact item license before importing: https://kenney.nl/assets/building-kit

Do not represent the generated world as containing Kenney meshes. They are optional follow-up art-direction resources.
