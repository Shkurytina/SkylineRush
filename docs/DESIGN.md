# Skyline Rush — product brief

**Genre:** casual Roblox Simulator + Tycoon. **Target audience:** players who want a readable 30-second loop, progression, and optional social comparison.

**Core loop:** collect → sell → invest → unlock a new zone → get more value per bag → upgrade your generator → repeat.

**Onboarding:** spawn into visible 3D world with bright crystals, clear sell / upgrade prompts and a top-left HUD. No purchase is required to play.

**Longer-term loop:** three crystal-value zones; owned tycoon plots; ten generator levels; ten speed levels; thirteen carry tiers; scoreboards of coins and collected resources.

**Monetization:** deliberately not active in this portfolio prototype. Potential future cosmetic gamepasses, private servers and optional convenience purchases must be tested for fairness, moderation compliance and actual demand. No revenue, retention, CCU or ARPU claims are made.

**Success metrics if published:** tutorial completion rate, first-sale time, first-upgrade time, D1 / D7 retention, median session duration, conversion to first generator purchase.

**Scope exclusions:** proprietary Roblox content, third-party copyrighted graphics, paid models, UGC products, external APIs and monetization receipts. All visible assets are generated from Roblox built-in primitives at server start.
